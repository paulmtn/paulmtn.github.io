ME, PAUL

I'm currently a second year student studying Computer and Electrical Engineering. I was born in California and currently live in West Virginia. My favorite color is viridian green!

I started getting into drawing and painting in my classes in high school and although I decided to not major in art, I try to keep up my art as much as I can in my free time. You can check out some of my paintings in the gallery on my site. I'm inspired especially by impressionists and others like John Singer Sargent, Monet, and Degas. I love how they paint boldly with large brushstrokes with everything coming together in a beautiful painting once you stand back.

I want to become an Embedded Systems Engineer and am interested in PCB design, firmware, and bare metal C. The project I'm currently working on is a custom keyboard from scratch.

I love listening to japanese shoegaze, korean shoegaze, and kpop. I like to play Factorio and League of Legends. I like reading sci-fi books such as the Three Body Problem and the Culture Series.
